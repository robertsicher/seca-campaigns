SCALE / Multi-Site

Prove it locally. Scale it nationally.

A national rollout should be the result of evidence, not the starting assumption. Select representative sites, establish baselines, test the programme and build a rollout decision around what actually changes.

Pilot → Proof → Rollout

A six-phase deployment model.

PHASE 1

Select 2 to 5 representative sites

Choose locations that reflect meaningful operator segments rather than only the easiest sites.

PHASE 2

Establish baseline metrics

Document utilisation, PT funnel, engagement, retention and customer feedback before intervention.

PHASE 3

Deploy Progress You Can Prove

Introduce the appropriate member journey, training and campaign activation.

PHASE 4

Measure

Track utilisation, repeat scans, PT introductions, conversion, engagement, retention where appropriate and customer feedback.

PHASE 5

Compare

Use baseline or suitable control locations to understand what changed and where.

PHASE 6

Build rollout business case

Decide whether economics, operational adoption and customer response justify wider deployment.

Campaign Strategy

Interview mode

Objective

Create demand for TRU Alpha within UK health and fitness by connecting a measurable member problem to a commercial operator outcome.

Audience

Premium operators first, specialist and performance gyms, then larger national groups where a pilot can build evidence for rollout.

Campaign model

Problem → content → calculator → HubSpot identification → nurture → demo → pilot → measurable commercial proof → rollout.

Measurement

Traffic → engaged target accounts → calculator completions → MQL → demonstrations → pilots → opportunities → pipeline → closed revenue → subsequent service/customer lifetime value.

The purpose is not to claim a commercial uplift before evidence exists. The campaign creates useful hypotheses, captures operator economics, then uses pilot data to validate what actually changes.
