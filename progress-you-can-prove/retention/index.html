PROTECT / Retention

Get more value from every member you acquire

The commercial cost of churn is more than lost monthly membership revenue. Operators may also need to invest again to replace members they have already paid to acquire.

Commercial hypothesis: if making progress more visible influences engagement, coaching participation or retention, what could that change be worth? The calculator below models scenarios. It does not claim TRU Alpha causes the improvement.

Commercial storytelling

What is invisible progress costing?

Without a visible progress loop

Member joins → gym spends to acquire them → member trains → bodyweight changes little → progress is difficult to see → motivation may decline → cancellation risk.

With a measurement-led review loop

Member joins → baseline TRU Alpha measurement → goal → training programme → follow-up measurement → progress conversation → next goal.

The hypothesis to test is whether making progress more visible can influence engagement, coaching participation or retention.

Test it rather than assume it

Design a 90-day pilot

Define a cohort, establish baseline engagement and retention indicators, introduce a structured measurement-and-review journey, then compare repeat measurement, coaching engagement, qualitative feedback and retention where statistically appropriate.

Campaign Strategy

Interview mode

Objective

Create demand for TRU Alpha within UK health and fitness by connecting a measurable member problem to a commercial operator outcome.

Audience

Premium operators first, specialist and performance gyms, then larger national groups where a pilot can build evidence for rollout.

Campaign model

Problem → content → calculator → HubSpot identification → nurture → demo → pilot → measurable commercial proof → rollout.

Measurement

Traffic → engaged target accounts → calculator completions → MQL → demonstrations → pilots → opportunities → pipeline → closed revenue → subsequent service/customer lifetime value.

The purpose is not to claim a commercial uplift before evidence exists. The campaign creates useful hypotheses, captures operator economics, then uses pilot data to validate what actually changes.
