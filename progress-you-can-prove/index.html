Beyond the scales

Make the work visible.

Training progress is rarely one number. A member can become stronger, gain muscle or reduce body fat while their total bodyweight changes very little.

Progress You Can Prove turns measurement into a repeatable member journey: establish a baseline, make the result understandable, connect it to a goal and come back to see what changed.

For operators, that creates a more useful question than “do we need another piece of equipment?” The question becomes where could a better progress journey create value in our business?

Behind the campaign

Strategy notes

Commercial objective

Create demand for TRU Alpha by starting with an operator problem, not a product specification. Each route connects measurable progress to a different commercial priority.

Audience model

Premium operators and specialist performance facilities provide strong early use cases. Larger groups become an account-development opportunity once a pilot creates credible evidence.

Demand generation model

Problem-led contentBusiness-case toolFirst-party dataHubSpot identificationRelevant nurtureDemoPilotProofRollout

HubSpot architecture

Calculator values can populate Contact and Company properties, while page behaviour and form actions create intent signals. Nurture then branches by retention, PT, performance, premium or multi-site interest.

Fit + intent scoring

Fit could weight operator size, role and positioning. Intent could weight calculator completion, repeat product engagement, pilot content and demo requests. Weights should be validated against real opportunity creation.

Account-based marketing

Commercial, fitness, operations and procurement contacts from one operator should roll into one company-level opportunity rather than being treated as unrelated leads.

Measurement

Traffic → engaged target accounts → calculator completions → MQL → demonstrations → pilots → opportunities → pipeline → closed revenue → subsequent service and customer lifetime value.

Principle: the site does not assume TRU Alpha causes retention or revenue uplift. It creates a commercial hypothesis, captures the operator's economics and gives sales a structured route to validate the value through pilot data.
