Behind the campaign

Strategy notes

Commercial objective

Create demand for TRU Alpha by starting with an operator problem, not a product specification. Each route connects measurable progress to a different commercial priority.

Content by intent

Retention and PT use calculators where operator economics improve the conversation. Performance, premium and multi-site use practical downloadable assets because guidance is more useful than speculative modelling at those stages.

Demand generation model

Problem-led contentUseful tool or guideFirst-party dataHubSpot identificationRelevant nurtureDemoPilotProofRollout

HubSpot architecture

Calculator values can populate Contact and Company properties. Download behaviour identifies the subject an account is researching, allowing nurture to branch by retention, PT, performance, premium or rollout interest.

Fit + intent scoring

Fit can weight operator size, role and positioning. Intent can weight calculator completion, repeat product engagement, guide downloads, pilot content and demo requests.

Account-based marketing

Commercial, fitness, operations and procurement contacts from one operator should roll into one company-level opportunity rather than being treated as unrelated leads.

Measurement

Traffic → engaged target accounts → calculator completions / downloads → MQL → demonstrations → pilots → opportunities → pipeline → closed revenue.

Principle: the campaign should create useful commercial conversations without inventing certainty. Calculators use operator data where the maths is meaningful; guides help prospects design a practical use case before a sales conversation.
