For the interview

Behind the campaign

Four customer priorities create demand. Relevant demonstrations and evidence from pilots turn interest into a sales opportunity.

Start with a defined audience

Proposed launch: UK premium independent clubs and small groups with staffed onboarding and a coaching offer. Reach owners, general managers and heads of fitness. Validate operational fit before targeting larger estates.

Test two propositions first

Start with retention and PT through targeted LinkedIn activity, relevant search demand and permissioned email. Match the destination page to the message. Compare channels and creative by qualified demand, using source and campaign parameters.

A worked retention journey

  1. A fitness director arrives from a retention campaign and enters their club's numbers.
  2. For 3,000 members, £80 monthly membership revenue, 25% annual churn and a one-point improvement over six extra months, the scenario represents 30 retained members and £14,400 in associated membership value.
  3. A request for a discussion would pass the objective, source and scenario to a Contact and Company record. The scenario is an assumption to validate, not a promised return.
  4. Sales checks the current member journey, operational capacity, costs and timing, then agrees a demonstration or pilot where there is a credible opportunity.

Qualify fit and intent separately

A download alone is an engaged lead. Proposed MQL criteria combine a suitable UK operator and relevant role with a request for contact or a validated engagement threshold. Sales acceptance establishes an SQL; agreed scope and buying process establish an opportunity.

Use the context in follow-up

Deliver the requested resource, then provide relevant follow-up where appropriate. Route demonstration requests into an agreed sales response queue. Branch nurture by retention, PT, performance or premium interest. Use site count and operational needs to identify a pilot conversation.

Develop the account

Associate fitness, operations, commercial and procurement stakeholders with the Company record. Aggregate relevant engagement and coordinate the sales approach. Site count informs account development rather than creating a fifth campaign proposition.

Measure commercial progression

Track qualified demonstrations held, sales acceptance, pilot starts, opportunities, sourced pipeline and closed revenue. Use downloads and calculator engagement to diagnose the journey. Report influenced pipeline separately with agreed attribution rules.

First 90 days

Prepare: confirm the audience, product claims, CRM fields and sales handover. Test: launch two propositions with a capped budget agreed against deal economics. Decide: compare cost per qualified demonstration, progression to opportunity and sales feedback before reallocating spend.

A pilot establishes adoption, operational fit and early coaching conversion. Assess longer-term retention with an appropriate follow-up period and comparable cohorts.
